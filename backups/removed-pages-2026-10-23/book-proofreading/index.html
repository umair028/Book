PROOFREADING SERVICES

What’s Included in Our Proofreading Service?

We carefully proofread your manuscript for grammar, spelling, punctuation, formatting, consistency, and overlooked errors. Our final quality check ensures your book reads smoothly, looks professional, and meets the standards expected by readers and publishers.

Typo & Spelling Check

A close read for lingering typos and spelling errors, including ones that slipped back in after layout like a character name that reverts to an earlier spelling.

Grammar & Punctuation Check

A final pass for grammar and punctuation issues that a copyedit may have missed, or that appeared during text reflow after formatting.

Formatting & Layout Check

A review of the laid-out file itself; widow and orphan lines, inconsistent spacing, page numbers on chapter openers, and headers appearing where they shouldn’t.

Consistency Check

A check for consistency in names, timelines, capitalization, and styling throughout the manuscript, since small drifts are easy to miss without a dedicated pass.

HOW WE WORK

How Your Final Proofread Comes Together

Your final proofread at BES Publisher is completed through multiple quality checks. We review every chapter, eliminate overlooked mistakes, and confirm consistency so your manuscript meets professional publishing standards before release.

STEP 01

FILE REVIEW

We confirm your manuscript has already been through copyediting and layout, since proofreading works best as the very last step, not a substitute for earlier editing.

STEP 02

PROOFREADER MATCH

Your manuscript is assigned to a proofreader experienced in your genre, and you receive a written agreement covering scope, pricing, and timeline.

STEP 03

PROOFREADING PASS

Your proofreader works through the laid-out file, marking corrections without making structural changes that could disrupt your formatting.

STEP 04

FINAL DELIVERY

You receive the corrected file along with a summary of what was fixed, ready for final upload or print.

FAQ’S

Questions Authors Ask About Book Proofreading

Clear answers to the questions authors ask most before starting book proofreading services with BES Publisher.

Copyediting happens earlier and focuses on grammar, style, clarity, and consistency across the full manuscript, generally taking several weeks. Proofreading happens after layout, on the near-final formatted file, and focuses narrowly on catching remaining typos, punctuation slips, and formatting errors introduced during typesetting; it isn’t a substitute for the deeper work copyediting does.

Yes, generally. Copyediting and layout often introduce new errors; text reflow, font substitution, or a correction that reverts during formatting that only a dedicated post-layout proofread will catch. Skipping this step means those layout-stage errors go straight to publication.

Some professionals offer both, but many authors get better results using a separate person for each stage, since a fresh set of eyes is more likely to catch errors the original editor’s eyes have already adjusted to.

Proofreading is generally the lowest-cost editing stage, since its scope is narrower than a full copyedit, and it typically has a faster turnaround as a result; though exact pricing and timeline depend on manuscript length.
