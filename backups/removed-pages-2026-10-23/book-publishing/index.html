PUBLISHING SERVICES

The Technical Work Behind Every Published Book

Getting a manuscript market-ready involves more technical detail than most first-time authors expect; file specifications, ISBN registration, metadata, and platform requirements all have to be right before a book can go live. Here’s what we handle.

Editing & Proofreading

Developmental editing, line editing, and proofreading passes that catch structural issues, tighten prose, and eliminate the typos that undermine a reader’s confidence in your book.

Interior Formatting & eBook Conversion

We format your manuscript to meet the technical specifications of Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, and EPUB/MOBI standards, so your book displays correctly on every device and platform.

Cover Design

Custom front, spine, and back cover design for both print and digital formats, built to read clearly at thumbnail size and stand out in your genre’s category listings.

ISBN Registration & Distribution

We handle ISBN registration, Library of Congress details, and distribution setup across major retailers and library platforms, so your book is actually available for readers to buy.

FAQ’S

Questions Authors Ask About Publishing Services

Clear answers to the questions authors ask most before starting book publishing services with BES Publisher.

Book publishing services provide the editing, design, formatting, and distribution work an author needs, while the author retains full ownership, rights, and royalties. A traditional publisher instead acquires rights to your manuscript and pays an advance and royalty share in exchange for control over the publishing process.

An ISBN is required for most print and many eBook sales channels, since retailers and libraries use it to catalog and track your book. Some platforms, like Amazon KDP, offer a free ISBN, though it will be listed under the platform’s imprint rather than your own.

Yes. Publishing services can typically be used individually; formatting alone, editing alone, or cover design alone, rather than requiring a full package if your manuscript has already had other steps completed elsewhere.

Timelines vary by scope, but a manuscript needing full editing, design, and formatting typically takes two to four months from start to publication, while formatting and distribution alone can often be completed in a few weeks.
