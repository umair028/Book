Backup of the home page taken before the "modern touch-up" (2026-10-23).

To undo the touch-up:
1. Copy index.html from this folder back to the site root (replacing the new one).
2. Optionally delete assets/css/home-modern.css and assets/js/home-modern.js
   (only the new home page uses them).

style.css, responsive.css and custom.js are saved here too for reference;
the touch-up does not change them.

Do not upload this "backups" folder to the live website.
