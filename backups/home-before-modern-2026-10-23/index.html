RANKED #1 PUBLISHING SERVICES

Everything Your Manuscript Needs to Become a Book

Publishing a book involves more moving parts than most first-time authors expect: editing, design, formatting, and getting the finished product in front of readers. BES Publisher brings every one of those services together under one roof, so you’re working with a single team instead of juggling freelancers.

Book Editing & Proofreading

Our editors review your manuscript line by line for grammar, punctuation, pacing, and clarity, then proofread the final draft so nothing slips through before it reaches print or digital shelves.

Ghostwriting

If you have a story to tell but not the time or writing background to draft it yourself, our ghostwriters interview you, study your voice, and write the manuscript on your behalf with full ownership rights transferring to you.

Book Formatting

We format your manuscript to meet the technical specifications of Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, and other print and eBook platforms, covering interior layout, margins, chapter breaks, and front/back matter.

Book Cover Design

Your cover is often the first and sometimes the only impression a reader gets before deciding to buy. Our designers build covers that reflect your genre and stand out in a crowded marketplace, in print and thumbnail size alike.

We believe authors deserve communication that feels human, guidance that makes sense, and a team that shows up when they say they will. Here’s exactly what happens after you reach out, step by step, with no surprise fees or vague timelines along the way.

STEP 01

INITIAL CONSULTATION

We start with a conversation about your manuscript, your goals, and your timeline. This is where we figure out which services you actually need, not a package sold to everyone regardless of their book.

STEP 02
